Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 7 days of receiving their order.

Refunds are available only when the item arrived damaged, the item is defective or does not work, or a different item was delivered. Refunds are not available if the customer simply changed their mind.

Refunds can be requested only after the order has been delivered.

Only orders that were paid successfully can be refunded.

Orders that have already been refunded cannot be refunded again.

Once a refund is approved, it is normally processed within 5-7 business days.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method.
